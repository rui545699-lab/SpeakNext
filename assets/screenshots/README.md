# Product Screenshots

These screenshots are from the working WeChat Mini Program used during product validation. They show representative stages of the product flow and are not presented as one continuous user session.

1. `01-reading.png` — reading material before the first retelling;
2. `02-concept-intervention.jpg` — one grounded Concept intervention;
3. `03-targeted-retry.jpg` — a target-expression retry instead of a full retelling;
4. `04-successful-progress.jpg` — a result where the original training target improved;
5. `05-partial-improvement.jpg` — a result where one issue improved but the overall task remained incomplete.

The images are product evidence for the V0.5.10.3 stable baseline. They do not imply quantitative effectiveness validation.
